Title: How ICT Changes Our Society

Create a 3–5-minute educational video explaining how Information and Communication Technology (ICT) affects our everyday lives and society.

  • How ICT changes communication.
  • How technology affects education and employment.
  • Positive and negative effects of ICT.
  • Responsible use of technology.

Website display: Embed the video from YouTube or another accessible video platform.

ICT Awareness Video

3–5 MINUTES
Watch on YouTube

What You Will Learn

This video discusses four important topics about the influence of ICT in our modern society.

01. Communication

ICT makes communication faster and easier through social media, instant messaging, emails, and video calls. People can now communicate with others around the world without needing to be in the same place.

02. Education & Employment

Technology helps students access online learning materials, attend virtual classes, and improve their knowledge. It also creates job opportunities and allows people to work using digital tools and online platforms.

03. Positive & Negative Effects

ICT provides many benefits, including faster access to information, easier communication, and improved productivity. However, it can also lead to cyberbullying, online scams, misinformation, and privacy problems.

04. Responsible Use of Technology

Responsible technology use means protecting personal information, respecting others online, avoiding suspicious links, checking information before sharing, and using the internet safely and positively.

Conclusion

In conclusion, Information and Communication Technology has become an important part of our everyday lives. It helps us communicate with other people, access information, study, and find different job opportunities. Technology also makes many tasks easier and faster. However, despite its advantages, ICT also has negative effects such as cyberbullying, online scams, and the spread of false information. As users of technology, we should learn how to protect our personal information, respect others, and think carefully before sharing anything online. Overall, ICT can make our society better when we use it wisely, safely, and responsibly.
