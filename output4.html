INTRODUCTION

The Science of Movement

Newton's laws help us understand why objects move or stay still. We can see these laws in simple activities like playing basketball, pushing a shopping cart, and jumping.

01 / NEWTON'S FIRST LAW

Law of Inertia

WHAT IS THE FIRST LAW?

The first law means that an object will stay still if nothing makes it move. If an object is already moving, it will keep moving until a force changes its motion.

REAL-LIFE EXAMPLE: BASKETBALL

A basketball will not move by itself while it is on the court. A player needs to push or kick it to make it move. When the ball rolls, the court slowly slows it down because of friction until it stops.

02 / NEWTON'S SECOND LAW

Law of Acceleration

WHAT IS THE SECOND LAW?

The second law explains that the way an object speeds up depends on how much force we use and how heavy the object is. When we push an object harder, it can speed up more. But a heavier object needs more force to speed up.

F = m × a

F = Net force | m = Mass | a = Acceleration

REAL-LIFE EXAMPLE: SHOPPING CART

Imagine pushing a shopping cart. If you push it gently, it moves and speeds up slowly. If you push it harder, it speeds up more. This shows that using more force can make the same cart accelerate more.

WHAT HAPPENS?

When you gently push the cart, it moves a shorter distance. When you push it harder, it moves farther in the same amount of time. This shows how a stronger force can cause greater acceleration.

03 / NEWTON'S THIRD LAW

Action and Reaction

WHAT IS THE THIRD LAW?

The third law means that forces always come in pairs. When one object pushes or pulls another object, the other object pushes or pulls back with the same amount of force but in the opposite direction.

REAL-LIFE EXAMPLE: PERSON JUMPING

When a person wants to jump, their feet push the ground downward. At the same time, the ground pushes the person upward. This upward push helps the person leave the ground and jump into the air.

CONCLUSION

Understanding Motion

Newton's three laws help us understand how things move around us. Through the examples in this presentation, we can see that pushing, stopping, and jumping all involve forces. These laws are not only found in science lessons but also in our everyday activities.

🏀

FIRST LAW - INERTIA

An object stays still until a force makes it move. A moving object keeps moving until a force changes its motion.

🛒

SECOND LAW - ACCELERATION

Pushing an object harder can make it speed up more. Heavier objects need more force to get the same acceleration.

🧍

THIRD LAW - ACTION AND REACTION

When one object pushes another, the second object pushes back with the same amount of force in the opposite direction.
