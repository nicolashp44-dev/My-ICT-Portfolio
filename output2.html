Short Description

This digital poster promotes the importance of being a responsible internet user. It presents five DO's and five DON'Ts that can guide people in using the internet safely and properly. It reminds everyone to protect personal information, respect other users, avoid suspicious links, and think carefully before sharing anything online. Through this activity, I learned that being a responsible digital citizen means using technology wisely and helping create a safer and more positive online environment.
